Hello! We are delighted you’re considering a stay with us. To make sure Triskelion is the right fit for your trip, please review the details below. 🙏

Context

This is our family home since 1960. We added 2 modern rooms to offer our guests a clean, pollution-free stay with green and pleasant surroundings.

Please note this is a homestay, not a hotel. We run it alongside our regular jobs, so we appreciate your understanding of our house rules and of what to expect and what not to.

Location & photos

We are in Gimhavane, Dapoli. The sea shores are roughly 7–8 km away, and we are just 1.5 km from the Dapoli ST stand.

Facilities

Both rooms have an attached washroom, A/C, Smart TV (Netflix), Alexa, water heater and high-speed Wi-Fi.

Know before you go

  • Meals: we do not provide lunch or dinner, but there are many excellent restaurants nearby. To help us manage waste, we kindly discourage bringing parcels back to the rooms. If you do, please ask us for extra bags to segregate the trash.
  • Power backup: an inverter runs one fan and one light per room. A/C and TV need the main grid. Power outages are common on Mondays and during the monsoon.
  • Drivers: we do not have separate accommodation for drivers.
  • Smoking and alcohol: smoking is strictly prohibited on the premises. You are welcome to enjoy drinks privately in your room, provided it does not disturb other guests.

How to find us

Coming from Dapoli ST stand: pass Patwardhan Hospital (on your right), then look for Madhu Milind Pharma (on your left). Take the immediate next left. We are less than 25 metres in. Look for the lamps on our gate and a long open corridor in front of the gate, with our house on the right and the 2 rooms on your left.
